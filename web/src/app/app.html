Rules
  • Men move one square diagonally forward. They capture forward or backward by jumping an adjacent enemy piece.
  • Capturing is compulsory, and a piece that starts capturing must keep capturing while it can.
  • A man only becomes a king if its move ends on the far row.
  • Kings fly any distance along a diagonal, and capture at any distance too.
  • You lose if you have no legal move. 50 quiet moves in a row (no capture, no man move) is a draw.
