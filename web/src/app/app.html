Rules
  • Men move one square diagonally forward, and capture forward only, by jumping an adjacent enemy piece.
  • Capturing is optional, but a piece that starts capturing must keep capturing while it can.
  • Nfikh: if your opponent could have captured and didn't, tap the piece that could have captured to take it off the board, then make your move. Only on the very next turn.
  • A man only becomes a king if its move ends on the far row.
  • Kings fly any distance along a diagonal, and capture at any distance, in any direction.
  • You lose if you have no legal move. 50 quiet moves in a row (no capture, no man move) is a draw.
  • With a move timer, running out of time skips your turn. Three skipped turns in a row lose the game.
